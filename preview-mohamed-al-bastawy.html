Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach is student-centered, practical, and tailored to each learner’s level, age, and individual needs. I focus on making lessons clear, engaging, and easy to follow while encouraging students to participate actively rather than simply listen. I use a structured approach that combines explanation, practice, repetition, and regular feedback to help students make steady progress. I also create a supportive learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and making mistakes. My goal is not only to help students learn the Qur’an and Islamic Studies, but also to develop a deeper understanding and connection with their faith.
